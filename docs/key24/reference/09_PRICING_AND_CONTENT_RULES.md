# Pricing / proof / claims policy

- €399 / €699 / €1,249 are proposed monthly EUR prices + applicable taxes; show real contract currency and invoice amounts in live country checkout. Never auto-convert using a guessed exchange rate.
- Before public launch owner must approve per-tier included voice minutes, number of phone numbers, languages, users, WhatsApp allowance, API/CRM features, onboarding fees, cancellation, overages and support.
- 3–4 existing customers may provide powerful evidence but are not permission to display their name, logo, face, quote, company screenshot, or claimed revenue lift.
- If a metric is legitimate, publish exact measurement (e.g., "148 calls handled in July 2026 across one consenting pilot customer", with source and no implication this applies to everyone). Sample phrasing is NOT a true claim and must not be rendered unless backed by evidence.
- Calculators may use adjustable hypothetical inputs and label all outputs "illustrative estimate, not a prediction"; no invented default "average lost job" presented as research.
- Do not display an OpenAI/ChatGPT partnership badge without authorization; ad services are not a guarantee of placement or of influencing answers.
- Use explicit real demo requests rather than fake calendar availability, inflated urgency counters or countdowns.
- Validate cross-border marketing/privacy/consent separately; a working site does not authorize sending bulk cold emails.
