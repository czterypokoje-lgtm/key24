# Client proof: turn 3–4 real customers into credible proof

## Ask each client for permission and facts (not a made-up story)
- Written permission for legal business name, role, person photo, logo and an exact attributed quote.
- Which KEY24 modules they use and since when; what the workflow looked like beforehand.
- A specific result IF logs and dates support it: calls handled, inquiries categorized, team time saved from recorded process observation, response coverage. Define date range, denominator, exclusions, how data was counted. Do not claim causal revenue growth without reliable evidence.
- Optional 20–30s on-camera clip and a heavily redacted screenshot from the CRM.
- Consent scope (websites, email campaign, video), approval date and withdrawal process.

## Proof section layout
Use masonry inspired by provided screenshot, not copied text:
1. Featured approved customer quote in tall white card with portrait/logo if permitted.
2. Black spotlight metric tile with one verified metric including period/denominator, or an honest feature workflow diagram instead.
3. Customer quote in shorter white tile, with attribution.
4. "See inside KEY24" product workflow card (can be published even without review consent, provided screenshot properly sanitized).
5. Detailed case study card linking to `/customers/[slug]` ONLY if approved story exists.

## Gate
`publish=false` on unapproved records; testimonials list filters on written release + attribution + claim verification. No dummy reviews on published pages. Do not mislabel staged demos as client results.
